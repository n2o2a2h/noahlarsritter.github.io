My Story

My Artistic Journey.

  • 2003-2021

    My Beginnings

    I have always been drawn to creative expression and storytelling, whether I was making iMovies or drawing comic books. I practiced with passion and grew from it, which set me up with a desire to keep growing and keep practicing.

  • 2021-2025

    University of Denver

    At the University of Denver I was introduced to many more passionate people. I learned that collabortion is key and with that I worked with others to create many meaningful creative projects. I also learned many valuable skills through my talented professors.

  • June-August 2024

    Filmaking in Mexico

    Under the great hospitality and tutalage of Mexicana filmmaker Soco Aguilar, I helped contribute to a feature film and the production of the documentary Sandra Cisneros Uncensored. I traveled, I grew more in my craft, and was able to learn new skills in business, animation, and film-production.

  • August-December 2025

    Volunteering Across South America

    My most recent adventure came after my gradution. It was my first time in South America and I visited Chile, Brazil, Columbia, and Peru. Volunteering for hostels and a nature reserve brought me incredible opportunities and experiences. I was able to work with new people in different languages, helping with social-media and story-telling within business.
