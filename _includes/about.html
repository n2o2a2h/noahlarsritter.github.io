My Story

My Artistic Journey.

  • 2003-2021

    My Beginnings

    I have always been drawn to creative expression and storytelling, whether through making iMovies or drawing comic books. I practiced with passion and grew from the experience, which instilled in me a desire to keep learning and improving.

  • 2021-2025

    University of Denver

    At the University of Denver, I was introduced to many passionate people. I learned that collaboration is key, and with that mindset, I worked with others to create numerous meaningful creative projects. I also gained many valuable skills from my talented professors.

  • June-August 2024

    Filmaking in Mexico

    Under the excellent hospitality and tutelage of Mexicana filmmaker Soco Aguilar, I contributed to a feature film as well as the production of the documentary Sandra Cisneros Uncensored. I traveled, grew further in my craft, and gained new skills in business, animation, and film production.

  • August-December 2025

    Volunteering Across South America

    My most recent adventure came after my graduation. It was my first time in South America, where I visited Chile, Brazil, Colombia, and Peru. Volunteering at hostels and a nature reserve provided me with incredible opportunities and experiences. I worked with new people in different languages, contributing to social media and storytelling for business.
